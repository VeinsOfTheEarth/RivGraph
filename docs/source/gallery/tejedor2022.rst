The Entropic Braiding Index (eBI): a robust metric to account for the diversity of channel scales in multi-thread rivers
========================================================================================================================

*Alejandro Tejedor, Jon Schwenk, Maarten Kleinhans, Ajay B. Limaye, Lawrence Vulis, Paul Carling, Holger Kantz, Efi Foufoula-Georgiou*

.. rst-class:: rg-paper-meta

   Geophysical Research Letters (2022)

`Read the publication <https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1029/2022GL099681>`_

RivGraph was used to measure channel counts and widths in support of the Entropic Braiding Index, a multiscale metric for braided rivers.

How RivGraph was used
---------------------

RivGraph was applied to the Indus River to compute the Braiding Index and the Entropic Braiding Index at multiple cross sections.
